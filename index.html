Our story

She said yes in downtown Dallas.

Sean got down on one knee at Thanks-Giving Square, under the white spiral of the chapel, with the city’s glass towers looking on.

Afterward we walked the streets at golden hour and celebrated over cocktails at The Adolphus, the grand old hotel downtown. Next June we’re trading the skyline for the vines, and we’d love for you to be there.

Two seats at the table, and a lifetime to go.
